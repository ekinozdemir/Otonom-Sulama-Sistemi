To me, electronics, physics, music, and literature are different languages describing the same underlying universe. I am a maker who seeks intellectual satisfaction through the discipline of mathematics and the profound, quiet depth of philosophy.

My worldview is shaped by a broad global perspective, developed while traveling through 15 countries. This journey taught me to look at the world with a wide vision, transforming crowded streets into a vast laboratory for philosophical and systematic observation.

The Sonic & Technical Sentez:
My relationship with sound evolved from traditional roots to modern engineering. I started playing music with the Saz, a traditional Turkish instrument, which deeply shaped my understanding of melody and microtones. My discovery of rock music shifted my focus toward the acoustic, electric, and bass guitars. Today, I combine this musical background with embedded systems—designing autonomous hardware solutions using Arduino, exploring sensory electronics, and producing digitally arranged music with philosophical weight.

I thrive in the quiet focus of deep technical execution, complex problem-solving, and the calm clarity of analytical thinking.
